User correction: remove the invented sticky-note slogans. Do not reintroduce “A little logic. A little magic.” or generic personality claims. Any new personal note wording needs to come from Gurleen.

Do not flatten the supplied mockup into the page or use a generated hero as the finished site. Match composition and atmosphere, with clear recruiting navigation and reduced-motion support.

The project claims shown inside the mockup (including NVIDIA evaluation metrics) are visual-reference content, not verified résumé evidence. Do not publish them as facts without owner confirmation or supporting source material.
